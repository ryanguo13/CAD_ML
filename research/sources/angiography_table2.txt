Table 2 Diagnostic documentation columns.
From: A large-scale 12-lead electrocardiogram dataset for acute coronary syndrome prediction containing 19,955 ECGs
Column name
Type
Description
Patient_id
String
Patient’s id.
ecg_row_record
String
The corresponding row ECG file name.
ecg_med_record
String
The corresponding median ECG file name.
gender
Integer
Gender information, with 1 representing male and 0 representing female.
age
Integer
Age of the patient.
Time_Interval
Integer
Records the time interval between ECG recording and coronary angiography, with the unit specified in minutes.
AMI
Integer
Indicates whether the patient has acute myocardial infarction. A value of 1 denotes the presence of the condition.
OMI
Integer
Indicates whether the patient has acute occlusion myocardial infarction, defined as acute coronary occlusion with TIMI flow grade 0-1. A value of 1 denotes the presence of the condition.
CTO
Integer
Indicates whether the patient has chronic total occlusion of the coronary artery, defined as a chronic (≥3 months), complete occlusion (TIMI flow grade 0 or 1). A value of 1 denotes the presence of the condition.
PCI
Integer
Indicates whether the patient has undergone percutaneous coronary intervention. A value of 1 denotes that the patient has undergone the procedure.
NSTEMI
Integer
Indicates an acute myocardial infarction with evidence of myocardial necrosis (elevated cardiac troponin values) but no persistent ST-segment elevation on ECG. A value of 1 denotes the presence of the condition.
STEMI
Integer
Indicates an acute myocardial infarction characterized by persistent ST-segment elevation on the ECG. A value of 1 denotes the presence of the condition.
UA
Integer
Indicates a clinical syndrome of myocardial ischemia without myocardial necrosis (cardiac troponin values not elevated), representing a high-risk precursor to acute myocardial infarction. A value of 1 denotes the presence of the condition.
LM
Integer
Left Main, the origin of the left coronary artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes Chronic Total Occlusion (CTO).
PLAD
Integer
Proximal LAD, proximal segment of the left anterior descending artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
MLAD
Integer
Mid LAD, middle segment of the left anterior descending artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
DLAD
Integer
Distal LAD, distal segment of the left anterior descending artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
DB
Integer
Diagonal Branch, a branch of the left anterior descending artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
PLCX
Integer
Proximal LCX, proximal segment of the left circumflex artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
MLCX
Integer
Mid LCX, middle segment of the left circumflex artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
DLCX
Integer
Distal LCX, distal segment of the left circumflex artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
OM
Integer
Obtuse Marginal Branch, a branch of the left circumflex artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
PRCA
Integer
Proximal RCA, proximal segment of the right coronary artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
MRCA
Integer
Mid RCA, middle segment of the right coronary artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
DRCA
Integer
Distal RCA, distal segment of the right coronary artery. A value of 1 denotes significant stenosis with acute occlusion in OMI cases; a value of 2 denotes CTO.
VF_VT
Integer
Indicates presence of ventricular fibrillation or ventricular tachycardia
Paced
Integer
Indicates paced rhythm
Prior_PCI
Integer
Indicates history of prior percutaneous coronary intervention
